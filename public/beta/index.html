Get Junoon on your phone

Part 2

How to send feedback

Something broke, confused you, or made you smile? Tell us from TestFlight.

Best for showing us a screen.

  1. Take a screenshot while you are in Junoon.
  2. Tap the little preview, draw on it if you like, tap Done.
  3. Tap Share Beta Feedback, add a note, tap Submit.

Best for a thought or an idea.

  1. Open the TestFlight app and tap Junoon.
  2. Tap Send Beta Feedback. Add a screenshot if you have one.
  3. Write your note and tap Submit.

Arjav will be scheduling a 5 to 10 minute call with you once per week to get your direct feedback.

That's it. Come back to Part 2 anytime you have feedback.
